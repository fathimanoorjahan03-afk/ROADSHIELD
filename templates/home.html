ABOUT ROADSHIELD

Turning Collision Data into Meaningful Insights

ROADSHIELD is a road safety analytics platform developed using historical UK road collision data. It combines machine learning, statistical analysis, and data visualization to help users explore collision severity and geographical patterns.
