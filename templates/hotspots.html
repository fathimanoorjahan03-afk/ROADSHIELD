How Hotspots Are Identified

ROADSHIELD groups geocoded Fatal and Serious collision records into 1 km × 1 km geographical cells. Slight collisions are excluded.

The map highlights cells whose severe collision counts are at or above the 99th percentile among occupied cells. These are candidate historical hotspots, not confirmed future accident locations.

These counts represent reported collisions, not the number of people killed or injured. They are not adjusted for traffic volume, so hotspot concentration should not be interpreted as an independent measure of collision risk.
